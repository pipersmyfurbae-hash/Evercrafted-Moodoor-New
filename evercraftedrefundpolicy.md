# Evercrafted — Refund Policy

**Effective date:** [date] · **Last updated:** [date]

## The short version

Because your blueprint is a digital file delivered instantly, we generally can't offer refunds once it's been downloaded — the same standard most digital-goods sellers use. But we know these purchases can carry real emotional weight, especially memorial and sympathy designs, so we've built in specific, fair exceptions below rather than a flat no-refunds policy.

## You're eligible for a full refund if:

- **You haven't downloaded or accessed the blueprint yet**, and you contact us within [X hours/days] of purchase asking to cancel
- **The file is genuinely defective** — it won't open, is missing a promised component (preview image, placement guide, story, shopping list, or instructions), or fails our own quality gate in a way that shouldn't have shipped
- **We made an error** — wrong design delivered, wrong size, or a technical mistake on our end

## You're eligible for a design adjustment (not necessarily a refund) if:

- **A floral listed in your Locked blueprint is discontinued** and the substitution we made (see our Terms, Section 7) doesn't work for you — we'll work with you on an alternative before you build, or issue a partial refund reflecting the difference if we can't resolve it
- **Your memorial/sympathy order needed our human review step** and that added meaningful delay — we'll always disclose this at checkout, but if it caused a real problem (e.g. missing a specific date), tell us and we'll make it right, which may include an expedite, a partial refund, or both depending on the situation

## What isn't covered by a refund:

- Change of mind after downloading a working, correctly-delivered blueprint
- Dissatisfaction with the aesthetic outcome once you've built it yourself — we can't guarantee a subjective result, though we're always glad to hear feedback and will take it seriously for future designs
- The cost of florals or materials you purchased to build the design
