# Evercrafted — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [date] · **Last updated:** [date]

## 1. Our Approach

Evercrafted asks people to share something personal — a feeling, a memory, sometimes a story about someone they've lost — in order to design something meaningful for them. We take that seriously. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how long we keep it.

## 2. What We Collect

**Directly from you:**
- Your intake responses: the feeling or occasion you describe, details about the person or moment (which may include names, relationships, and health/loss information you choose to share), color preferences, wreath size, optional inspiration photos
- Contact information: email address, and name/shipping details if applicable
- Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe — we receive confirmation of payment, not your card number

**Automatically:**
- Basic technical data (browser type, general location from IP, pages visited) for security and to keep the site working correctly

## 3. How We Use It

- To generate your blueprint: your intake responses are processed to produce the emotional interpretation, floral selection, placement, and written narrative
- To process and deliver your order
- To communicate with you about your order (confirmations, delivery, and — for memorial/sympathy orders — our human review step)
- With your separate opt-in consent only: to send you occasional design inspiration or new blueprint announcements

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your intake content — including anything you share about a loss or a person you're honoring — for advertising or marketing purposes.**
